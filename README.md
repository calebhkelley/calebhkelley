### Hi, I’m Caleb

I’m a data scientist and computational linguist. I build end-to-end data pipelines, train machine learning models, and turn unstructured text and speech into clear, usable findings.

I’m currently a Visiting Assistant Professor at Emory University, where I teach Spanish linguistics and use computational methods to study language variation and identity. I hold a Ph.D. in Romance Languages from The University of Alabama.

### Featured Project

#### [retail-review-nlp](https://github.com/calebhkelley/retail-review-nlp)

NLP analysis of 568K Amazon product reviews. Sentiment analysis with VADER, LDA topic modeling, and supervised classification with logistic regression and random forest pull clear signals out of unstructured customer feedback.

### Research

My academic work brings computational methods to sociolinguistics. I study how phonetic variation carries social meaning, with a focus on gender, sexuality, and cultural identity in Spanish-speaking communities.

`Variationist sociolinguistics` `Hispanic phonetics` `Computational approaches to language variation` `Second language acquisition` `Educational technology`
